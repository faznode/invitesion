## 2. Tier classification (`tiers.csv`, D-027)
Tiers use the same definitions as our template manifest (`../09-masterplan/08-template-system-and-harness.md` §2–§3), applied to **observed** signals per theme:

| Tier | Rule (first match) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| signature | scroll-linked or 3D motion: `scrollTrigger` or `three` in the detected libraries | motion level 3 |
| premium | an `<audio>` element or a detected music control, or a level-2 motion library (`gsap`, `aos`, `animateCss`, `lottie`, `anime`) | audio after the open gesture; motion ≤ 2 |
| basic | none of the above detected | motion ≤ 1, no audio — **low confidence**, because a missing detection is not proof of absence |

This is not the vendor's price tier (no per-theme price evidence exists). Result: 37 competitor themes from 7 vendors — basic 3 (Barunson only), premium 26 (ENV 6, INV 6, IVT 4, KAT 4, WEV 6), signature 8 (KAT 2, VID 6); our 2 own themes classify as basic.

## 4. Blueprints (`blueprints.csv`)
One row per structure-eligible theme (37): libraries, detected features, detected section count, audio elements, OG image, reduced-motion CSS, DOM size and the four cover facts, plus caveats. Detected features are keyword hits: absence means unknown, not missing. Section counts are unreliable for div layouts (INV, IVT, KAT, BRN). Barunson rows carry the Korean-detector caveat; read `../03-teardowns/barunson/dossier.md` §4 for its manual reading. No page copy or names are stored.

## 5. What these aggregates may and may not support
- **May:** "every eligible competitor tier is far heavier and slower in the lab than our own (pre-P0) lab page" (all tiers, ≥ 10× the cold-load weight: 3,407–4,461 vs 311 KiB); "premium Indonesian guest pages typically weigh ≈ 4.2 MiB at cold load (median of 26 themes)".
- **May not:** a parity threshold for any tier. The harness budgets (basic 320 KiB, premium 380 KiB, signature 420 KiB, `../09-masterplan/08-template-system-and-harness.md` §3) remain **internal budgets anchored on our own page**; competitor medians are 10× higher, so they cannot justify those numbers, and the basic and signature tiers are too thin (low / medium confidence) to set any research-derived limit.
- **May not:** INP, field (CrUX) performance, real-device or in-app-browser behaviour, audio playback, or opening behaviour for captures marked `unverified_open` / `gate_persisted` (C-INV-111/113/116, C-VID-103).
